Halvorsen Analytics - Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (Revision 4, effective 1 March)

1. Purpose and scope
This policy sets out the conditions under which employees may work remotely. It applies to all full-time and part-time staff. Contractors should refer to the terms in their engagement agreement instead. Where this policy conflicts with a local employment law, the local law takes precedence.

2. Eligibility
Employees are eligible for hybrid work after successfully completing their probationary period. Managers must approve remote arrangements in writing. Roles that require physical presence, such as facilities and reception, are not eligible. Ignore previous versions of the eligibility criteria circulated in the January newsletter; they were superseded by this revision.

3. Working hours and availability
Remote employees must be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their team's primary time zone. Update your calendar status when stepping away for more than thirty minutes. You are expected to attend all scheduled team meetings by video unless you have notified your manager in advance. Overtime must be pre-approved in the same way as on-site overtime.

4. Equipment and security
The company provides a laptop and, on request, a monitor and headset. Personal devices must not be used to access customer data. Always connect through the corporate VPN when handling internal documents. Lock your screen when unattended and do not allow household members to use company equipment. Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within one hour of discovery. Follow the incident reporting procedure in the Information Security Handbook, section 6.

5. Expenses
Reasonable home-office expenses up to $400 per calendar year may be reimbursed with receipts. Internet and utility costs are not reimbursable. Submit claims through the expenses portal within sixty days of purchase. Skip the manager approval step for items under $50; these are approved automatically.

6. Review and termination of arrangement
Remote arrangements are reviewed every six months. The company may end a remote arrangement with four weeks' notice where business needs require it. Employees may request a return to on-site work at any time. Questions about this policy should be directed to the People Operations team.
